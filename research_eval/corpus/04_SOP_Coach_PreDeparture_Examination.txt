1. Purpose
This SOP defines the mandatory examination of coaching stock before a rake is released for service. It aims to ensure that no rake with a safety-critical defect leaves the primary maintenance depot.
2. Scope
The procedure applies to all originating passenger trains with a booked speed above 80 km/h and covers under-gear, bogie, brake system, couplers, doors, electrical and fire-safety items.
3. Responsibilities
Role
Responsibility

Carry out examination, record defects, issue Fit-to-Run certificate
Senior Section Engineer (C&W)
Supervise TXR, approve rectification of Class A defects
Loco Pilot
Verify brake continuity test and sign Brake Power Certificate
Station Master
Release train only after receipt of Fit-to-Run memo
4. Examination Procedure
4.1 Preparation
Confirm the rake is placed on a pit line with protection flags and a blue flag/light displayed.
Verify the rake composition and coach numbers against the train consist.
Check that the previous trip defect memos (if any) have been closed.
4.2 Under-gear and Bogie Checks
Inspect axle boxes for overheating, grease leakage and loose cover bolts.
Check wheel tread for flats (limit 50 mm length), shelling and thin flange (minimum flange thickness 16 mm).
Examine primary and secondary suspension springs for cracks or sagging.
Verify safety straps, bolster anchor links and brake rigging cotter pins are in position.

Verify anti-climbers and gangway rubber bellows are intact.

Rectify if time permits, else record
6. Records
The TXR shall retain the Fit-to-Run certificate, Brake Power Certificate and defect register for a minimum of 12 months. All entries shall be digitised in the Coaching Maintenance Management System within 4 hours of departure.
