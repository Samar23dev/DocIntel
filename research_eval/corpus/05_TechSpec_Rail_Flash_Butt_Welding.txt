Technical Specification: Flash Butt Welding of Rails
Specification No.: ENGG/TS/WELD/2026/05     Issue Date: 20 September 2026     Status: Draft for review
1. Introduction
This specification covers the requirements for flash butt welding (FBW) of 60 kg/m, 90 UTS rails to form long welded rail (LWR) panels at a fixed welding plant. It defines materials, equipment, process parameters, inspection, and acceptance criteria.
2. Referenced Standards

IRS T-59: Flash butt welding of rails

6. Inspection and Testing
6.1 Visual and Geometric
Straight-edge test (1 m): vertical deviation 0 to +0.3 mm on the running table.
Lateral deviation of gauge face not to exceed 0.3 mm over 1 m.
No visible cracks, notches or burn marks on the weld collar.

7. Documentation
Each weld shall carry a unique identification marked on the web, comprising welding plant code, date, machine number and serial number. Machine data logs shall be retained for the service life of the rail panel.
8. Non-Conformance
Any weld failing a prescribed test shall be cut out and rejected. Where a failure is found in a sample test, all welds produced since the last satisfactory test shall be 100% re-tested.
