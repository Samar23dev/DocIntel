 3. Pantograph Maintenance Procedure (IA Schedule)
 3.1 Safety precautions. Ensure the locomotive is stabled on a track with OHE isolated and earthed.
 Obtain a Permit-to-Work (PTW) from the Traction Distribution (TrD) controller. Lower the pantograph and
 confirm using the roof inspection platform. Isolate the main circuit breaker (VCB) and apply the key
 interlock.
 3.2 Carbon strip inspection. Measure remaining carbon thickness at five points along each strip.
 Replace the strip if thickness is less than 8 mm at any point, or if cracks exceed 20 mm in length, or if there
 is a step wear greater than 3 mm.
 3.3 Contact force. Using the calibrated dynamometer, record static contact force at working height 4.5 m.
 Acceptable range is 70 N +/- 10 N (rising) and 60 N +/- 10 N (falling).
