                       RAIL WEAR MONITORING REPORT
    Head wear versus cumulative tonnage on two monitored locations

    Document No.                    ENGG/RAILWEAR/2026/07
    Rail section                    60 kg/m, 90 UTS, LWR
    Measurement method              Rail profile gauge, every 50 GMT
    Locations                       Straight Km 5-9; Curve (R=650 m) Km 13-14

    1. Wear Trend
    Vertical head wear was measured at fixed stations at every 50 GMT of traffic. The curved location shows
    markedly faster wear than the straight track because of higher lateral wheel-rail forces.
