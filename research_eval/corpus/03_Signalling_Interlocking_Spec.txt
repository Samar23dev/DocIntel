       SIGNALLING & INTERLOCKING TECHNICAL
                  SPECIFICATION
 Electronic Interlocking (EI) for Major Station Yard

 Specification No.                  S&T/EI/SPEC/2026/017
 Station                            Sample Junction (SMJ) - 6 platforms, 42 points, 58 signals
 Safety Integrity Level             SIL-4 (EN 50126 / 50128 / 50129)
 Issued By                          Signal & Telecom Engineering Dept. (fictional)

 1. System Overview
 The Electronic Interlocking shall control all points, signals, track circuits and axle counters within the
 station limits. The system shall employ a 2-out-of-2 (2oo2) vital processor architecture with hot-standby
 redundancy and shall conform to the Railway Board approved interlocking principles. All route setting shall
 be through VDU-based operator control with a fallback panel interface.

 2. Functional Requirements
 Req. ID         Requirement                                                                             Verification
 FR-001          Route shall be set only if all points are proved locked in required position            Simulation + site test
 FR-002          Conflicting routes shall never be set simultaneously                                    Interlocking table check
 FR-003          Signal shall revert to danger within 1.0 s of track circuit occupation ahead            Timing test
 FR-004          Route release timer for emergency cancellation: 120 s (main), 60 s (shunt)              Functional test
 FR-005          Approach locking for main signals, with 180 s release                                   Functional test
 FR-006          All events logged with 10 ms timestamp resolution, retained 90 days                     Audit

 6. Reliability Targets
 System availability shall be not less than 99.995%. Mean Time Between Wrong-Side Failures (MTBWSF)
 shall exceed 109 hours. Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) shall not exceed 2 hours for any replaceable
 module.
